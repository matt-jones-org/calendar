Black Ink

How often a name sat at the top of the leaderboard.

In an old newspaper stat page, leading the league got your line set in bold — black ink. The Black Ink Test turns that into a score: weighted points every time a hitter finished first in a category. Tally a career below.

The idea

Bold = led

Every league-leading finish once got printed in bold type. More bold, more dominance.
