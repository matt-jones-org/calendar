The 20–80 scale

Scouting Report

Five tools, one honest grade.

Scouts grade every tool on a 20–80 scale, where 50 is big-league average and every ten points is a standard deviation. Below is a report in that language — a template you can drop any player into.

The card

Tool Grades

Present grades are what a player shows today; the OFP (Overall Future Potential) rolls the projected tools into a single number.

Sample Prospect
SS · R/R · 6'1", 190 · Age 21 · Double-A
55

Field50

Report

Advanced feel to hit from the right side with a compact, repeatable stroke and the strength to punish mistakes to the pull side. Chase rate ticked down in the second half — the plate discipline is real, not a small-sample mirage.

Actions at short are steady rather than flashy; average range plays up on a plus, accurate arm. Fringe-average runner underway, but reads and first-step quickness keep him at the position long-term. Everyday shortstop with a chance for more if the power fully arrives.

Scale — 20 well-below-average · 40 below · 50 average · 60 plus · 70 plus-plus · 80 elite.

Why 20–80

Bell curve

Centered on 50 with 10-point steps mapping to standard deviations — a common language across every org.

Present vs future

Two numbers

What a player is now and what he projects to be. The gap is the bet a team is making.
